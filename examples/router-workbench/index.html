How this choice is made

Required constraints filter the catalog first. The canonical v2 router then ranks eligible models using your objective and selects only from its Pareto frontier.

A model is outside that frontier when another eligible model is no worse in quality, reliability, health, cost and latency, and strictly better in at least one of them.

All model facts are caller-supplied estimates, not measured performance, current provider quotes or permission to execute.
