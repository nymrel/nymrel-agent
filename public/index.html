BUILT FOR MULTI-PROVIDER TEAMS

A routing decision your team can inspect.

01

Constraints before scores

Models that violate risk, boundary, modality, context, cost, or latency requirements are rejected before ranking.

02

Visible tradeoffs

Choose balanced, quality, cost, or latency. Component scores show exactly why the winner moved.

03

Low-custody by default

The hosted service accepts routing metadata only. The optional customer-local harness makes provider calls in your environment with your provider account.

04

Receipts without bodies

Local runs produce correlation digests, events, route facts, and usage without placing task or output text in receipts.

THE V0.1 BOUNDARY

Useful now. Honest about what is next.

The public product routes; it does not execute model calls. The local harness executes read-only requests through configured adapters. Write-capable tools, hosted provider-key custody, billing, and autonomous side effects are outside this release.
